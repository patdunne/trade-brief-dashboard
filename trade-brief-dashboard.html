Deal tracker

Who has cut a deal with Washington, who is stuck, and who is still at the table.

Vietnam

Done

Deal agreed: 20% tariff on Vietnamese goods entering the U.S., plus U.S. aircraft and farm product purchases. Final deal coming soon.

40% tariff on transshipped goods

United Kingdom

Done

Deal sealed at the Kananaskis G7: lower tariffs on cars under a quota, exceptions for aerospace, and a commitment to negotiate pharma, steel and aluminum later.

Cars · aerospace · more to negotiate

Japan

Stuck

The 25% auto tariff is a dealbreaker. Trump is unhappy and threatening more tariffs; a U.S. ask to raise defence spending to 5% of GDP is also on the table.

Autos · defence spending

South Korea

Stuck

Still stuck — Seoul is debating dropping antitrust laws and increasing defence spending toward 5% of GDP (from 2.3%) to get a deal done.

Antitrust · defence spending

India

In talks

In a tough spot: U.S. demands on agriculture — especially genetically modified crops and dairy — are politically sensitive. India needs a win it can sell at home.

Agriculture · GM crops · dairy

European Union

In talks

Moving cautiously: a possible deal on non-tariff barriers such as digital rules. If talks fail, retaliation is on the table — tariffs, or even a new trade club without the U.S.

Non-tariff barriers · retaliation possible
